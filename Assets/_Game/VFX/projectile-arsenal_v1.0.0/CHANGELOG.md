# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-04

- Reworked the bullet: it now flies straight, nose first, and spins about its own axis (rifling bands and a corkscrew wake, 8-frame loop).
- Every projectile loop and every laser segment joint verified pixel-exact.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-03

- All directional projectiles and muzzle flashes drawn natively in 8 directions.
- Added the 64x64 premium edition, drawn at native resolution.
- Fixed a jump in the shock orb loop.
- Muzzle flashes and rocket smoke no longer touch the cell edge.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-02

- Added tracer, ice shard, shock orb, homing helix, sword wave, acid glob, rocket and bullet.
- Ten colour variants for every projectile, energy muzzle flash, hit burst and laser.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-01

- First projectiles: plasma bolt, fireball, arcane disc.
- Pistol, rifle and energy muzzle flashes, hit bursts and a three-part modular laser beam.
